Create a modern, premium and minimalist personal website for a beginner freelance web developer named:

MATEUSZ WOJTERA

This website will be my personal business card and the main place where potential clients can learn about me, see my work and contact me.

IMPORTANT CONTEXT:

I am just starting my freelance web development business.

Do NOT make me look like an established agency.

Do NOT invent:
- years of experience
- number of clients
- completed projects
- testimonials
- awards
- companies I worked with
- fake statistics
- fake customer reviews

The website should instead present me honestly as a new freelancer who is building his portfolio and taking on new projects.

The goal is to make a visitor think:

"Mateusz is starting out, but his work looks professional and I can contact him about my website."

- small businesses

- freelancers
- creators
- service providers
- small online businesses

- online stores

- websites with user accounts
- booking systems
- blogs
- newsletters
- various integrations
